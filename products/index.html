What We Supply

Quality Oil & Gas Equipment

We supply a comprehensive range of oil & gas equipment, safety products and petroleum accessories sourced from reputable manufacturers and delivered to client specifications.

Air Compressor

Industrial-grade reciprocating and rotary screw air compressors for oil & gas and petrochemical applications. Available in various capacity ranges.

Cylinder Carrier

Heavy-duty LPG cylinder carriers for safe and efficient transport and distribution of gas cylinders across distribution networks.

Cylinder Carrier (Extended)

Extended-capacity cylinder carriers designed for large-volume LPG distribution with improved load-bearing capacity.

Foam Mobile Unit

Portable foam fire-fighting units for rapid emergency response at petroleum depots, LPG plants and industrial facilities.

Excess Flow Valves

Automatic safety valves that shut off gas flow when flow exceeds a set threshold, protecting pipelines and equipment from rupture.

LPG Storage Vessels

LPG storage bullets and tanks from 2.5MT to 120MT capacity, designed and manufactured to ASME and BS PD5500 standards.

Gas Regulating Equipment

High-quality pressure regulators, safety relief valves and gas flow measuring equipment for LPG and natural gas systems.

Safety & PPE Equipment

Comprehensive range of personal protective equipment and safety accessories for petroleum and oil & gas work environments.

Need a Specific Product?

We can source and supply specialised oil & gas equipment on request. Contact our products team to discuss your requirements.
