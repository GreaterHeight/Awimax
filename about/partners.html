Certifications & Standards

Accreditations & Compliance

DPR Licensed

Awimax Energy Limited is fully licensed by the Department of Petroleum Resources (DPR) to operate as an EPCIC contractor and petroleum products supplier in Nigeria.

View Certificate (PDF)

Quality Management

We maintain rigorous quality management processes aligned with ISO 9001 standards across all aspects of our engineering, procurement and construction activities.

CAMA Registered

Registered under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) as a fully Nigerian-owned and incorporated company, committed to indigenous participation in Nigeria's petroleum industry.

Supply Chain

Our Supply Partners

We work with reputable international and local suppliers to source the highest quality materials, equipment and components for our EPCIC projects. Our supply chain partnerships ensure project delivery without compromise on quality or timelines.

Key supply categories include LPG storage vessels, pressure piping and fittings, fire protection systems, electrical and instrumentation equipment, civil and structural materials, and safety equipment.

We are always open to establishing new supply partnerships. If you supply quality oil & gas equipment and would like to become an approved Awimax supplier, contact our procurement team.
