Our CSR

Giving Back to the Communities We Serve

The Awimax Foundation is the corporate social responsibility (CSR) arm of Awimax Energy Limited, established to invest in the human capital, education and wellbeing of communities across the oil & gas producing regions of Nigeria.

We believe that a company's legacy is measured not only by the projects it builds, but by the communities it uplifts. Through the Foundation, we channel resources into programmes that create lasting change in the lives of young Nigerians and host communities.

Our Programmes

Foundation Programmes

Graduate Internship Programme

We provide structured internship placements for recent graduates and final-year students in engineering, project management, finance and administration. Interns gain hands-on experience working on real EPCIC projects under the mentorship of our senior engineers and managers.

Technical Skills Development

Our technical training programme provides vocational and hands-on skills training for young men and women from oil & gas communities in welding, pipefitting, electrical installation and industrial safety — equipping them to participate meaningfully in Nigeria's energy sector.

Education Support

Awimax Foundation provides educational grants and scholarships to academically deserving students from low-income backgrounds, with a focus on students studying engineering, sciences and technical disciplines at Nigerian universities and polytechnics.

Community Development

We invest in the physical and social infrastructure of communities neighbouring our project sites — including renovation of schools and clinics, provision of clean water access, and support for local small businesses and cooperatives.

Women in Energy

We are committed to increasing female participation in Nigeria's energy sector. Our Women in Energy initiative provides mentoring, networking and access to career pathways for women pursuing technical and professional roles in oil & gas.

Environmental Awareness

Through workshops, clean-up campaigns and community education events, we promote environmental stewardship in the regions where we operate, raising awareness of responsible oil & gas practices and sustainable living.
