Full-Spectrum EPCIC

Integrated Services Across the Oil & Gas Value Chain

From initial feasibility through to final commissioning, Awimax Energy delivers end-to-end EPCIC services that ensure your project is completed on time, within budget, and to international standards.

Service 01

LPG Plant Construction

We design, procure, construct, install and commission LPG storage plants ranging from 10MT skid-mounted units to large 500MT bulk storage facilities. Our comprehensive service covers:

  • Civil and structural engineering works
  • LPG storage vessel supply and installation
  • Pressure pipework and fittings
  • Safety valve systems and emergency shut-off
  • Loading and unloading gantries
  • Vapouriser and regulating systems
  • Final commissioning and operator training
Service 02

Construction & Storage Tank Farm

We deliver turnkey storage tank farm construction, covering the full scope from site preparation through to final handover. Our tank farm projects include:

  • Above-ground and underground storage tanks
  • Petroleum product tank farms (PMS, AGO, DPK)
  • Bitumen and chemical storage facilities
  • Tank calibration and certification
  • Dyke and bund wall construction
  • Product metering and dispensing systems
  • Fire suppression and foam systems
Service 03

Consultancy & Value Management

Our engineering consultancy team provides independent advisory, project management and value management services to operators and investors across the oil & gas sector:

  • Feasibility studies and concept design
  • FEED (Front-End Engineering Design)
  • Project planning and scheduling
  • Cost estimation and budgeting
  • Value engineering and cost optimisation
  • Regulatory compliance advisory
  • Project management and oversight
Service 04

Electrical & Instrumentation

We provide complete electrical engineering, instrumentation, automation and control systems services for petroleum facilities, including:

  • HV and LV electrical installations
  • Power generation and distribution systems
  • Process instrumentation and control
  • SCADA and automation systems
  • PLC programming and integration
  • Cable management and tray installation
  • Hazardous area classification and compliance
Service 05

Fire-Fighting Systems

Safety is non-negotiable in petroleum operations. We design and install comprehensive fire protection systems compliant with NFPA, API and DPR standards:

  • Fire detection and alarm systems
  • Foam and dry powder suppression systems
  • Hydrant and hose reel networks
  • Diesel-driven fire pump installations
  • Deluge and sprinkler systems
  • Gas detection systems
  • Emergency shutdown (ESD) systems
Service 06

Marketing & Sales of Products

Beyond construction, Awimax Energy is an active trader and distributor of petroleum products and oil & gas equipment across Nigeria:

  • LPG supply and distribution
  • Petroleum products (PMS, AGO, DPK)
  • Bitumen supply
  • LPG cylinders and accessories
  • Air compressors and industrial equipment
  • Fire-fighting equipment and consumables
  • Safety equipment and personal protective equipment
