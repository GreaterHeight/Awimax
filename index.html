About Awimax

A Leading EPCIC Company Serving Nigeria & Beyond

Awimax Energy Limited is a fully integrated Energy company with extensive experience in Engineering, Procurement, Construction, Installation and Commissioning (EPCIC) in the Oil & Gas industry across Nigeria and West Africa.

We provide comprehensive solutions spanning LPG plant construction, storage tank installations, fire-fighting systems, electrical & instrumentation works, and project consultancy — all executed to international standards.

LPG Plant Construction

Construction & Storage Tank Farm
